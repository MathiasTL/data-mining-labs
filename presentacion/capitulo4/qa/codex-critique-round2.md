# Codex critique, round 2 (codex exec, read-only, 7 contact sheets)

- **Slide 1:** The P1–P3 bars look like a scaled timeline, but only the OOT row shows week endpoints. Their lengths are hard to interpret.
- **Slide 4:** The Home Credit case-study strip sits below the three constraint cards and reads like a footer. The real case is easy to miss in the 4.1 argument.
- **Slide 5:** The LightGBM card has substantially more empty space below its text than the other two model cards, weakening the stepped comparison.
- **Slide 6:** The four-column literature table has little separation between rows. Long citations and explanations are difficult to track across the page.
- **Slide 12:** The chart repeats the six Gini values already in the adjacent table. Together they compress the configuration column, which carries the selection rationale.
- **Slide 18:** Seven table columns force several metric headers onto multiple lines, making the two segment rows harder to compare at a glance.
- **Slide 25:** The closing motif drops the P1, P2, P3 and OOT labels used on the cover, so the four bars lose their meaning as validation windows.

## Decisions (round 2)
Applied: closing motif labelled P1, P2, P3, OOT (25); case-study strip moved directly under the data figures (4); slide 18 headers shortened so no word breaks.
Rejected: slide 1 extra week ticks (label 'semana 0' and 'semana 91' present; the P1-P3 ends are the stated windows on slide 7); slide 5 empty space in the LightGBM card (intentional staircase); slide 6 row rules (booktabs style kept); slide 12 chart/table redundancy (as above).
